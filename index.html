Wedding FAQs

• ARE THE CEREMONY & RECEPTION INDOORS OR OUTDOORS?

The ceremony is indoors, at Our Lady of Annunciation. The reception and the party
are outdoors, whereas the afterparty moves inside to the glasshouse. Do bring
something warm, as January evenings in Dubai can be quite cold.

• WHAT IS THE PARKING STATUS AT BOTH VENUES?

Both self-parking and valet parking are available at Address Montgomerie. While
self-parking is also available at the ceremony location, spaces are limited. We highly
recommend arriving early to ensure you find a spot.
